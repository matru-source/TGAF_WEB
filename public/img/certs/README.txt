Certification logos
===================

Drop the 7 logo image files here, named EXACTLY as below (PNG, ideally
transparent background, ~400px tall). They then appear automatically in the
"Certified & trusted" section. Until a file exists, its card shows a text badge.

  son.png          Standards Organisation of Nigeria
  nafdac.png       National Agency for Food & Drug Admin. & Control
  halal.png        Halal Certification Authority (HCA)
  fda.png          US Food & Drug Administration
  man.png          Manufacturers Association of Nigeria
  nepc.png         Nigerian Export Promotion Council
  fssc-22000.png   Food Safety System Certification 22000

To change a filename or add/remove a logo, edit CERTS in lib/data.ts.
